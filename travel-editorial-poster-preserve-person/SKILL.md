# Travel Editorial Poster — Preserve Person

## Goal

Turn the user's photograph into a refined contemporary editorial art / travel poster while keeping every real person faithful to the original source image.

**Core rule:** Design the world around the person. Never regenerate the person.

The design may be highly creative. The person's identity may not be creatively reinterpreted.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Treat every real person as a **protected photographic subject**.
2. Never use generative image synthesis to redraw, beautify, reinterpret, reconstruct, or "improve" a person's face or body.
3. The final visible person must originate from the original uploaded photograph.
4. Never claim the face is unchanged unless the final person layer truly comes from the source image pixels.
5. Do not guess a city, country, landmark, date, or local language from visual association alone.
6. Use a place name only when supported by embedded GPS / EXIF metadata, clearly readable text/signage, a confidently identifiable landmark, or explicit user-provided context.
7. If location remains uncertain, omit the place name rather than inventing one.
8. When location is verified, prefer the official or primary local language of that place for the main poetic phrase or place-related typography. English may be secondary editorial typography.
9. If the photo contains no people, skip the person-removal and final person-compositing stages.

# WORKFLOW

## STAGE 0 — Inspect before editing

Inspect the original photograph for people, scene type, major motifs, readable text, metadata if accessible, likely subject location, lighting, perspective, and suitable output orientation.

If metadata is accessible, check it before inventing any place label.

If the location is not verifiable, design without a specific city/country claim.

Do not ask unnecessary questions if the poster can be made safely without the missing information.

## STAGE 1 — Create a clean person-free scene

If people are present, first create a clean scene with all people removed.

### Chinese instruction

請以我上傳的原始照片作為唯一基礎。

此階段只處理「移除人物與重建背景」。

請完整移除照片中的人物，包括臉部、頭髮、身體、衣服、手臂與手、腿與鞋子、包包、配件，以及明顯屬於人物的陰影或殘影。

人物移除後，根據周圍可見環境，自然補回原本被人物遮住的背景。

背景重建必須符合原照片的透視、光線方向、陰影、材質、色彩、景深與空間結構。

不要重新設計場景。不要任意移動建築、景物、道路、牆面、地面、植物或主要物件。不要改變原始攝影視角與場景邏輯。

最重要：完成後的畫面中不能存在任何人物。

不得留下人物殘影、臉部殘影、多餘手腳、衣服輪廓、人體輪廓、模糊人形、AI 補出的替代人物，或明顯的人物擦除痕跡。

此階段不要加入海報文字、插畫或裝飾。

輸出：一張與原始照片場景一致、但完全沒有人物的乾淨背景照片。

### English instruction

Use the uploaded original photograph as the only source image.

For this stage, perform only **PERSON REMOVAL AND BACKGROUND RECONSTRUCTION**.

Completely remove every person, including face, hair, body, clothing, arms and hands, legs and shoes, bags, accessories, and obvious person-specific shadows or remnants.

Naturally reconstruct the background hidden behind the person.

Keep the reconstruction consistent with the original perspective, lighting direction, shadows, materials, colors, depth, and spatial structure.

Do not redesign the scene. Do not arbitrarily move buildings, scenery, paths, walls, floors, plants, or major objects. Do not change the original camera angle or scene logic.

MOST IMPORTANT: the finished image must contain absolutely no people.

Do not leave ghost figures, facial remnants, extra limbs, clothing edges, body silhouettes, blurred human forms, generated replacement people, or obvious person-removal artifacts.

Do not add poster graphics, typography, illustration, or decoration during this stage.

OUTPUT: a clean version of the original scene with all people completely removed.

## STAGE 2 — Design the editorial poster with NO people

Use only the person-free scene as the creative image source.

### Absolute prohibition

Do not generate people, faces, bodies, silhouettes, pedestrians, blurred figures, or human-like forms.

If a person appears accidentally, remove it before proceeding.

### Art direction

Recompose the scene as a contemporary **EDITORIAL ART POSTER / EDITORIAL TRAVEL POSTER**.

The result should feel like a contemporary art book, independent design magazine, gallery-shop print, sophisticated travel publication, or modern Japanese / international editorial design.

Do not simply place the photograph inside a decorative frame.

Preserve selected photographic realism while integrating generous negative space, bold photographic cropping, irregular torn-paper boundaries, translucent color fields, layered paper, fine pencil lines, ink marks, restrained hand-drawn symbols, small botanical or architectural sketches, subtle colored-pencil or pastel texture, analog print grain, paper fibers, and selective printmaking texture.

The photograph, paper, graphics, and color fields must overlap and interact naturally.

Avoid the appearance of **"a photograph dropped inside a poster template."**

### Palette

Extract approximately **3–5 main colors from the original photo**.

Use a restrained and coherent palette. Do not force the same color palette on every image.

### Composition

Default output:
- 3:4 portrait
- high resolution
- contemporary editorial balance

Use strong negative space, off-center focal balance, asymmetrical editorial composition, confident cropping, layered scale, and occasional design elements crossing photo/paper boundaries.

Avoid generic postcard layouts, souvenir-poster styling, overly centered compositions, clutter, or retro-tourism styling unless specifically requested.

### Reserved subject area

Preserve enough visual space where the original person stood because the real source person will be returned later.

Do not place large headlines, critical typography, major illustrations, large opaque blocks, or essential information directly in that reserved region.

Design elements may approach or visually pass **behind** the future subject area so the final composition feels integrated.

Do NOT generate a replacement person.

## LOCATION & LANGUAGE RULES

### Location verification

Only use a city, country, landmark, or regional name when supported by GPS / EXIF metadata, clearly readable place text, a confidently identifiable landmark, or explicit user-provided location information.

Never guess a location from visual association alone.

Examples of forbidden logic:
- seeing a golden shachihoko and assuming Nagoya
- seeing an Eiffel-like structure and assuming Paris
- seeing generic Japanese architecture and inventing a city
- inferring an exact date from weather or seasonal appearance

If location cannot be verified, do not invent a place name.

### Language rule

When the location is verified, use the **official or primary local language** for the main poetic phrase and place-related typography when appropriate.

English may be used as secondary editorial typography.

If location is uncertain, do not invent a local language or city/country label.

### Main poetic phrase

Create one short phrase inspired by the image's memory, place, season, time, or emotional tone.

It must be poetic and understated, not explanatory.

Examples of tone:
- 「旅の途中。」
- 「また来ようね。」
- 「ここで見た空。」
- 「今日を、持ち帰る。」
- 「この景色を、覚えていたい。」

Do not mechanically reuse the same phrase every time.

## STAGE 3 — Return the ORIGINAL photographic person

Inputs:

A. Original photograph  
B. Completed person-free editorial poster

The final person must come directly from the original source photograph.

### Chinese instruction

現在進行最後的人物合成階段。

請從原始照片中精準擷取原本的人物。

人物必須直接來自原始照片。

不要重新生成、重新畫、重建臉部、美化、瘦臉、放大眼睛、改鼻子、改嘴型、改變表情、改變年齡感、重建皮膚細節，或建立「很像原本人物」的新角色。

保留原始人物的臉型、眼睛、眉毛、鼻子、嘴巴、耳朵、髮型、髮際線、皮膚、表情、頭部比例、身體比例、姿勢、手、腿、衣服、鞋子、包包與配件。

臉部尤其必須保持來源照片的真實影像。

將人物放回原照片相對應的位置、比例與姿勢。

只允許以下非生成式融合：
- 精準遮罩
- 極輕微邊緣羽化
- 人物外部接觸陰影
- 人物背後的圖形層次
- 背景色調調整
- 周圍紙張與印刷質感

不要重新生成或重繪人物內部像素，尤其不要修改臉。

設計元素可以從人物後方穿過或靠近人物，但不得重畫人物。

不得留下第二個人物、雙臉、重疊頭部、重疊手腳、AI 人體輪廓或人物殘影。

最終效果應該是：

**「原始照片中的真人，自然存在於完整 Editorial Art Poster 裡。」**

而不是：

**「一個由 AI 重新生成、只是很像原本人物的新角色。」**

### English instruction

This is the FINAL SUBJECT COMPOSITING stage.

Precisely extract the original person from the source photograph.

The person must come directly from the original photograph.

Do NOT regenerate, redraw, reconstruct the face, beautify, slim the face, enlarge the eyes, alter the nose, alter the mouth, change the expression, change apparent age, regenerate skin detail, or create a similar-looking replacement character.

Preserve the original subject's face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth, ears, hairstyle, hairline, skin, expression, head proportions, body proportions, pose, hands, legs, clothing, shoes, bag, and accessories.

The face in particular must remain the real photographic face from the source image.

Return the subject to the corresponding original position, scale, proportion, and pose.

Only non-generative integration is allowed:
- precise masking
- minimal edge feathering
- contact shadows outside the subject
- graphics behind the subject
- background tonal adjustment
- surrounding paper / print texture

Do not regenerate pixels inside the subject, especially the face.

Graphic elements may pass behind the person or approach the subject closely, but must not repaint the person.

Do not leave duplicate people, double faces, duplicate heads, duplicate limbs, AI-generated body halos, or ghost figures.

The final result must look like:

**"The real person from the original photograph naturally exists inside the finished Editorial Art Poster."**

It must NOT look like:

**"A newly generated character who merely resembles the original."**

# ABSOLUTE PRIORITY

The person is a **PROTECTED PHOTOGRAPHIC SUBJECT**.

Do not synthesize the person's identity.

The face and body must originate from the uploaded source photograph.

**All generative creativity must happen around the person, never to the person.**

If there is any conflict between artistic style and identity preservation:

**IDENTITY PRESERVATION ALWAYS HAS PRIORITY.**

# FINAL QA

Before delivery, verify all items below.

## Identity

- Final visible person comes from the original source photograph.
- No generative face reconstruction was applied.
- Face shape, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, expression, hairline, skin, and age appearance remain source-faithful.
- Clothing, body proportions, hands, legs, shoes, bag, and accessories remain source-faithful.
- No duplicate face, head, arm, hand, leg, shoe, bag, or silhouette remains.
- No AI-generated body halo remains behind the composited person.
- No person-shaped removal artifact remains in the background.
- Edge feathering is subtle; the subject must not look like a crude sticker.

## Scene

- Person-free background reconstruction is spatially plausible.
- Perspective and lighting are coherent.
- Architecture and major scene objects have not been arbitrarily moved.
- No accidental extra people were introduced.

## Location

- Any printed city / country / landmark is verified.
- No location was guessed from visual association alone.
- If location is uncertain, no unsupported place claim appears.
- Any date or season claim is known or written without pretending it came from metadata.

## Language

- Main local-language phrase matches the verified place's official or primary language when appropriate.
- English remains secondary unless the user requested otherwise.
- No fake or garbled local-language text is used as a major design element.
- Place-name spelling is correct.

## Design

- Poster feels integrated, not like a photo inside a template.
- 3–5 color palette is derived from the source photo.
- Negative space is intentional.
- Typography hierarchy is clear.
- Paper, grain, hand-drawn marks, and translucent forms are restrained.
- Original subject remains visually important.
- Design feels contemporary.

## Technical output

- Default ratio is 3:4 portrait unless the user requested another ratio.
- Final export is high resolution.
- Original uploaded file remains unchanged.
- If true pixel-preserving subject compositing could not be performed, do not falsely claim the person is unchanged.
